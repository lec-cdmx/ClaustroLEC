English version · Call for proposals

Mexico City Region Research Seminar

Dear colleagues, we are working on launching a research seminar with the Ciudad de México, Santa Fe, and Estado de México campuses. We want to share ongoing research and receive feedback from colleagues across campuses. We encourage submissions that have made meaningful progress and can still benefit from discussion, including work you plan to present at external conferences or seminars.

Senior Research Seminar

The proposed dates for February–June 2027 are February 10, March 3, April 7, and May 19, all on Wednesdays from 1:00 to 3:00 p.m. Faculty interested in presenting should send an extended abstract of up to 1,000 words to pliedo@tec.mx with the subject “Senior Research Seminar – Proposal.”

Junior Research Seminar

For this first edition, we are seeking student research completed before December 2026 so that students have results to present and discuss. The proposed dates are February 17, March 10, April 21, and May 26, 2027, all on Wednesdays from 1:00 to 3:00 p.m. Please encourage interested students to send an abstract to pliedo@tec.mx and marco.mendez@tec.mx with the subject “Junior Research Seminar – Proposal.”

For either seminar, please indicate your preferred campus and specify any dates when you cannot attend. If any of the four dates would work, please say so. We will finalize the schedule and host campuses once we know the level of interest and availability.

Best regards,
Research Seminar Organizing Committee
